# Translation & transcription projects — tracking file

Not a reading note. This file tracks primary sources surfaced in this project's research that
are candidates for future translation or transcription work, separate from the project's own
publication (Series I-V). Two distinct categories, kept separate below:

1. **Untranslated** — Latin/German (or otherwise non-English) primary sources with no English
   translation at all, or only a stalled/unpublished one. A translation project.
2. **Out of print / no quality modern edition** — English-language primary sources that exist
   only as old-spelling facsimiles, raw OCR print-on-demand reproductions, or unedited EEBO-TCP
   transcriptions. A transcription/typesetting/republication project. Flagged as candidates for
   **Berith Press**.

Status markers follow the project's usual discipline: CONFIRMED (verified via direct
catalog/publisher check), WELL-ATTESTED (secondary-source consensus, not independently
verified), UNVERIFIED (a lead, not yet checked).

---

## 1. Untranslated primary sources

### Wolfgang Musculus

- ***In ambas apostoli Pauli ad Corinthios epistolas commentarii*** (Basel, 1559; 1566 Basel
  reprint also attested). Dedicated verse-by-verse commentary on 1 & 2 Corinthians — covers
  1 Cor 11:2-16 directly. **CONFIRMED to exist and be digitized** (period Latin scan via Google
  Play Books; documented in CERL and library catalogs). No English translation or modern
  critical edition exists. **High priority** — this is the right primary source for Musculus's
  1 Cor 11 material, which this project has not yet been able to access any other way.
- ***In Epistolas Apostoli Pauli ad Galatas et Ephesios commentarii*** (Basel, 1561; reprinted
  1569). Dedicated commentary on Galatians and Ephesians — covers Eph 5:22-33 at far greater
  length than the brief aside in his *Loci Communes* already in use. **CONFIRMED to exist** via
  library/bibliographic record; not yet located as a clean digitized text. **High priority**,
  same reasoning as above.

### Peter Martyr Vermigli

- ***In Primum Librum Mosis Commentarii*** (1569; from 1542-47 Strasbourg lectures) — Genesis
  commentary, covers Gen 1-3 including 3:16. An English translation (ed. John Patrick Donnelly,
  Peter Martyr Library) was announced by the Davenant Institute in 2017. **UNVERIFIED/stalled**
  — not among the nine volumes Davenant's own current PML page lists as completed; publication
  status unresolved as of this project's last check (2026-08-08).
- **1 Corinthians commentary** — covers 1 Cor 11:2-16. English translation by Jon Balserak was
  under contract with Truman State University Press, status "Accepted/In press" per a
  University of Bristol research-output record (2020). **UNVERIFIED/stalled** — six years
  unconfirmed as of this project's last check, and Truman State UP exited the Peter Martyr
  Library series in 2018 (transferred to Davenant); whether this volume transferred or was
  dropped is unconfirmed.
- *Note*: a live, unrelated translation project exists for Vermigli's *Loci Communes* (Davenant,
  trans. Kirk Summers) — vol. 1 (*On Original Sin*) is published, but no marriage-relevant
  section has appeared or been scheduled. Not a current translation target; worth monitoring.

### Theodore Beza

- ***Tractatio de repudiis et divortiis*** (Geneva: Vignon, 1569; reprinted in *Tractationum
  Theologicarum*, vol. 2, 50-103). Beza's systematic treatise on divorce, written as Calvin's
  successor extending and systematizing Geneva's marriage law. **Never translated into
  English.** Known to this project only via John Witte Jr.'s pinpoint-cited quotation of one
  passage (on desertion as a divorce ground). Full Latin text located on Internet Archive and
  Google Play Books. **High priority** — the clearest single next target for a Latin-reading
  pass on this project's Strand 1 marriage-law material, since the fuller argument (beyond the
  one quoted passage) is entirely unknown to this project.

### Martin Bucer

- ***Martin Bucers Deutsche Schriften*, Band 10: *Schriften zu Ehe und Eherecht*** (Gütersloh,
  2001; ed. Stupperich, Neuser, Seebaß, Strohm et al.) — the dedicated critical-edition volume
  of Bucer's German marriage-law writings: 19 items spanning 1524-1543, including the "Scriptum
  maius vom Ehegericht" (1534-37), the actual founding document of the Strasbourg marriage
  court reform. **Never translated. This is the single most important untranslated primary
  source this project has identified for Strand 1's civil/legal material.** Freely digitized,
  page-by-page with OCR, at the Heidelberg Academy of Sciences: digi.hadw-bw.de/view/bds10/0048.
  **Highest priority.**
- ***De Regno Christi*** (1550), Book II, chs. ~22-46 (marriage and divorce). A modern critical
  Latin edition exists (Wendel, *Opera Latina* vol. XV, 1955), but the **only modern English
  translation** (Pauck, *Melanchthon and Bucer*, LCC vol. XIX, 1969) **omits these chapters
  entirely** (WELL-ATTESTED via two independent secondary reports, not yet checked against a
  physical copy of the LCC volume). Effectively untranslated for this project's purposes — see
  also §2 below re: Milton's mediated 1644 digest, which is not a substitute.
- **Correspondence** — *Correspondance de Martin Bucer* / *Bucers Briefwechsel* (Brill, ongoing
  critical edition, vol. IX reached 2013). Specifically flagged: the volumes covering
  late 1531-1532, which likely contain correspondence on the Oecolampadius/Wibrandis
  Rosenblatt/Capito remarriage chain. Never translated; access likely requires
  library/subscription (Brill), not freely digitized.

### Girolamo Zanchi

- ***De Operibus Dei*** (*Operum Theologicorum*, Tomus III), Caput 2, "*De Matrimonio*"
  (cols. 820-832). A separate, systematic/hexameral treatment of marriage distinct from the
  already-translated *De Spirituali Connubio*. **Never translated.** A 1649 OCR scan exists on
  Internet Archive but is badly corrupted (much of the Latin rendered as garbled Greek Unicode)
  and appears to be the wrong tome regardless (correspondence/miscellanea, not this volume).
- ***In... epistolas ad Philippenses, ad Colossenses, ad Thessalonicenses commentarius***
  (Neustadt, 1595) — from Zanchi's Neustadt lecture series; almost certainly treats Col
  3:18-19, per the confirmed lecture sequence, but the passage itself has not been read.
  **Never translated.**
- ***De divortio deque novis post divortium nuptiis*** — bibliographically attested (Geneva,
  1617?) but its existence as a standalone title, rather than a section of the 1617-19 *Operum
  Theologicorum*, is **UNVERIFIED**. If real, never translated. Needs a primary-catalog check
  (PRDL/WorldCat) before this is even confirmed as a distinct translation target.

### Heinrich Bullinger

- Detlef Roth, ed., ***Pastoraltheologische Schriften*** (*Heinrich Bullinger, Werke*, 3.
  Abteilung, Bd. 5; Theologischer Verlag Zürich, 2009) — the modern critical edition of
  Bullinger's marriage writings, including the **previously-unknown 1527 *Vollkommne
  underrichtung des christenlichen eestands*** (an earlier, shorter marriage tract this project
  had not identified before this edition surfaced it) alongside a critical text of *Der
  christlich Eestand* (1540) itself. **Never translated into English.** Note: this is distinct
  from *The Christen State of Matrimony* (Coverdale's 1541 English translation of *Der
  christlich Eestand*), which already exists in period English — see §2 below for that item's
  different problem (out of print, not untranslated).
- **Correspondence** (Bullinger Digital, Universität Zürich) — searchable digitized archive of
  ~2,000 letters Bullinger wrote and up to 12,000 he received. Almost entirely untranslated
  (Latin/Early New High German). No specific marriage-counsel letter has been identified yet —
  this is a lead for a future search pass, not a confirmed translation target.

### Secondary scholarship (not a primary source, but blocks primary-source access)

- Walther Köhler, ***Zürcher Ehegericht und Genfer Konsistorium***, 2 vols. (Leipzig,
  1932-1942) — the standard critical study of the Zurich marriage court's actual caseload.
  German only. No English translation. This is currently the only known route to Zurich
  case-level material (parallel to what Jeffrey Watt's monograph provides for Geneva), so it
  blocks a whole category of Strand 1 evidence, not just one text.

## 2. Out of print / no quality modern edition — candidates for Berith Press

English-language primary sources where the content is already in English but the only
available forms are old-spelling facsimiles, raw print-on-demand OCR reproductions (Forgotten
Books and similar services, which reproduce scanning errors uncorrected), or academic
transcriptions not typeset for general readership. A proper modernized-spelling, edited,
introduced edition would be new value-add, not mere translation.

- **Heinrich Bullinger, *The Christen State of Matrimony*** (trans. Miles Coverdale, 1541;
  1552 London imprint). **No modern print edition exists at all** — the only accessible form is
  the 1552 facsimile scan on Internet Archive. This is one of the most historically significant
  texts in this project's Strand 1 bibliography (the most widely-read marriage manual in
  Edwardian/Elizabethan England) and has real, substantial wife's-side content already
  confirmed by this project's research (the "help or arm of man" material). **Strong Berith
  candidate** — old-spelling original with real content, zero modern editions competing.
- **Daniel Rogers, *Matrimoniall Honour*** (1642). Technically "in print" only via raw
  print-on-demand facsimile services (Forgotten Books and similar) that reproduce the original
  OCR uncorrected — **no properly edited, modernized-spelling edition exists**. Distinctive
  imago-Dei grounding for headship and a strong positive theology of companionate love; a good
  candidate for a real critical/reader's edition rather than a facsimile reprint.
- **Robert Cleaver & John Dod, *A Godly Form of Household Government*** (1598). No modern print
  edition located — only EEBO-TCP's raw transcription and a free online modernized-spelling
  scholarly transcription (Internet Shakespeare Editions), not typeset or published as a book.
  Contains the notable Abigail concession on female competence (already used in this project's
  Strand 2 notes) — a good, undersized text well-suited to a short standalone edition.
  **UNVERIFIED**: worth a final check that no press has quietly issued an edition since this
  project's last look.
- **Richard Steele, "The Duties of Husbands and Wives"** (Eph 5:33 sermon). Currently only
  hosted online (apuritansmind.com) — not published as a standalone print booklet anywhere
  located by this project, and the hosting site itself carries an uncorrected author-date error
  (misattributing it to Sir Richard Steele the essayist, 1672-1729, rather than the actual
  divine, 1629-1692 — worth fixing in any Berith edition's front matter). Short enough to make
  a natural pairing with another sermon/treatise rather than a standalone volume.
- **John Milton, trans., *The Judgment of Martin Bucer Concerning Divorce*** (1644). No
  standalone modern print edition located — only bundled into 19th-century (1820s) reprints of
  Milton's collected divorce tracts. **Caveat if pursued**: this is Milton's own selective,
  polemically-motivated translation/digest of Bucer's *De Regno Christi* — it should be
  published (if at all) explicitly as "Milton's Bucer," with the omissions and polemical framing
  noted in an introduction, not presented as a neutral rendering of Bucer's own argument. Lower
  priority than the items above for that reason — the untranslated *Deutsche Schriften* Band 10
  material (§1) is the more valuable target if the goal is Bucer's own voice.

### Lower-priority / needs more verification before recommending to Berith

- **Girolamo Zanchi, *The Spiritual Marriage...* (1592 English translation, Cambridge)** — a
  16th-century English translation exists independently of O'Banion's 2021 translation, but
  since O'Banion's modern edition already covers the same content and is in print, this is a
  historical-curiosity item (rare-book interest, original period reception) rather than a real
  content gap. Not yet located/read by this project. Low priority.
- **Thomas Cartwright, *Commentarii succincti et dilucidi in Proverbia Salomonis* (1617)** —
  belongs more naturally in §1 (it's Latin, not English) than here, but flagged specially: the
  only translation that exists is this project's own **non-professional working translation**,
  explicitly flagged for independent verification by a competent Latin reader before any public
  use. Real candidate for a proper commissioned translation, but needs that verification step
  first, not a straight transcription job.

## Open items

- This list currently reflects Strand 1 (and a few Strand 2 items) only, since that's the
  strand with a completed "expand" bibliography pass. Strands 3-6 haven't surfaced comparable
  untranslated/out-of-print candidates yet, mostly because those strands' core figures wrote in
  English — revisit this file as later strands get their own expand passes.
- None of the entries above have been vetted for copyright status beyond "pre-1923 = public
  domain in the US" as a rule of thumb; critical editions/introductions by living scholars
  (Roth, O'Banion, Selderhuis, etc.) are separately copyrighted even where the underlying
  primary text is public domain — check before using any modern critical edition as a
  transcription base.
