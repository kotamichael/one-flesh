## Who they are

CONFIRMED: Chad Van Dixhoorn (PhD, Cambridge) is professor of church history and theology (RTS Charlotte) and editor of the critical edition of the Westminster Assembly's minutes and papers. Emily Van Dixhoorn (MAR, Westminster Theological Seminary) leads Bible studies and women's retreats.

## What they argue (from interview content; book itself not yet directly reviewed by this research pass)

CONFIRMED (Crossway podcast): both affirm "equal dignity before God" alongside distinct roles — husband as head, wife as body. The book draws directly on **William Gouge** (per Reformed Forum's chapter timestamp "Gouge and Biblical Submission") and Augustine. Chad warns against husbands who "constantly talk about submission," which "flattens out the dynamics," and against the opposite error, denying differences, which produces "headless marriages." Emily, speaking in her own voice, reframes submission through an athletic image (head and body "work so beautifully together") and states her husband "doesn't force me to do things that I don't want to do."
